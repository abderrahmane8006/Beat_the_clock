Time Management Challenge

Can You Manage Your Day?

You have one very busy day.
Every task is mandatory.
Your goal is to organize your schedule, respect deadlines, and adapt to unexpected events.

HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Organize all mandatory tasks between 7:00 AM and 11:00 PM.

  • ✅ Complete every task
  • 🔒 Respect fixed schedules
  • ⏳ Respect deadlines
  • ⚠️ Avoid time conflicts
  • 🚨 Adapt to unexpected events
  • 👆 Drag cards onto the timeline. Drag them again to move them. Drop them back on the task list to unschedule.

YOU CANNOT DELETE ANY TASK.

The goal is not to do everything quickly.
The goal is to organize your time intelligently.
